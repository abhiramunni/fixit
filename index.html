The golden rule

Service before the season, not during it.

Mar 15

is the date that saves the most money in an Indian home — the last good day to service the AC before summer demand (and summer prices) arrive.

AC service in March: ₹800–1,200 with easy slots.
AC service in May: ₹1,500–2,500, a week’s wait, and a 10–15% higher power bill while you wait.
